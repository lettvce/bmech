FOR NONPROFESSIONAL USE ONLY

This is a free hobbyist/educational statics solver. It has not been verified, certified, or reviewed by a licensed engineer, and it is not intended for real structural design, construction, or any safety-critical use.

Do not rely on it for anything where being wrong could hurt someone or damage property. Use it to learn, check your own hand calculations, or experiment — nothing more.

Tools

  • Q Point — click empty space to place one (snaps to a grid).
  • W Member — click-and-drag from one point to another, or click, then click again.
  • E Cable — same as Member, but tension-only: it goes slack instead of carrying compression.
  • A Pin support — fixes X and Y at a point.
  • S Roller support — fixes one direction (pick vertical/horizontal in the sidebar).
  • D Fixed/welded support — fixes X, Y, and rotation (drawn as an X). Note: a truss's joints can't transmit moment, so this usually makes the structure indeterminate unless it's the only support.
  • F Load — set Fx/Fy in the sidebar, then click a point to apply it.
  • Section — click two points to draw a cutting line; solve the truss first.
  • Delete — click a point, member, or force arrow, or use the × buttons in the sidebar lists.
  • R Connect the two most recently placed points with a member — no clicking needed. Handy for chain-building: place, place, R, place, R, ...

Shortcuts are ignored while typing in any text field.

Coordinate readout

Hovering the canvas shows a small "(x, y)" tooltip at the cursor — snapped to the grid when the Point tool is active (matching exactly where a click would land), or the raw position otherwise.

Precise placement

While placing a Member/Cable, after picking the first point, use the "Precise member" panel to enter an exact Length and Angle instead of clicking a second point. Toggle Aligned / X-dist / Y-dist to dimension by the member's true length, its horizontal distance, or its vertical distance — all combined with the same angle.

Canvas navigation

  • Scroll wheel — zoom in/out, centered on the cursor.
  • Middle-click and drag — pan.

Editing

The Points / Members / Forces lists in the sidebar let you rename, reposition, and delete anything after the fact — no need to redraw.

Undo / redo

Ctrl+Z to undo, Ctrl+Y (or Ctrl+Shift+Z) to redo. Covers every action, including "Clear all".

Solving

Click Solve to get member forces (tension/compression) and support reactions, along with the equations used to find them. If the result is indeterminate, pure statics can't fully solve it — reactions may still be shown if they're independently solvable. If it's unstable, the structure is a mechanism under this load.
