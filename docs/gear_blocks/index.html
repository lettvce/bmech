How the spacing works

Every block stands on the same size base (80 mm wide by default). Two gears mesh when their axles are r₁ + r₂ + 1 mm apart — their pitch radii plus a 1 mm meshing clearance — so the gap between two stands is whatever's left: wide between big gears, narrow between small ones. Two very small gears can't sit next to each other at all, because their stands would overlap; the builder flags that.

  • Every block in a set must share the same Module (tooth size), Stand Height and Stand Width, or they won't line up and mesh. These live under Advanced; at the defaults, gears up to 58 teeth fit.
  • Below 17 teeth a 20° gear starts to undercut; it still turns, just with weaker tooth roots.
  • The gear is captive: the axle necks down through the gear and steps back out to a wider shoulder on both sides, with 0.3 mm print-in-place clearance. Print it with supports under the gear.
  • The tooth profile is the same involute bmech's spur gear generator makes in Blender.
